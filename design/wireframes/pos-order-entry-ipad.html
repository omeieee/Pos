Notes for review

  1. Tap a tile = add 1 with the item's default/last options (fast path). Long-press or tap the cart line opens the options sheet (B). Qty badge on tiles shows what's already in the order.
  2. Order panel is always visible on iPad (token layout.cartMode = panel, 380 px). Total and the คิดเงิน button never scroll away.
  3. Sold-out tiles stay in place (muscle memory) with a red "หมด" badge: colour + icon + text.
  4. The long item name wraps to 2 lines; tone marks on เลือด / น้ำตก have room (line-height 1.5 on names).
  5. The "รอตรวจสอบ 1" pill in the top bar is the claimed-payment queue (LINE "โอนแล้ว"). Tapping it opens that order's payment sheet.
