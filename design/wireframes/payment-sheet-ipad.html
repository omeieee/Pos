Notes for review

  1. The amount due is always the largest element (token fontSize.amount); in customer mode (B) it is 1.7× larger and readable at arm's length.
  2. Only the green "ยืนยันรับเงิน" button moves a payment to ชำระแล้ว. It never appears on the customer-facing screen, so a customer cannot tap it.
  3. Cash (A): quick tenders smaller than the total are disabled; change updates live; note breakdown is optional help.
  4. ไทยช่วยไทย (D): the full amount to type into ถุงเงิน is the hero; the government/customer split is labelled ประมาณการ (estimated). Scheme dates/hours come from settings; outside them the method is disabled with the reason.
  5. The PromptPay ID shown is a masked placeholder. The real ID comes from settings and is never in the design files.
