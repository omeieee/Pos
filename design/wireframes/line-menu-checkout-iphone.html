Notes for review

  1. Room number is pre-filled for returning customers (L2a), with a visible "เปลี่ยน" link. Reorder card sits at the top of the menu.
  2. ไทยช่วยไทย is shown but disabled for room delivery, with the reason in words. Picking "รับที่ร้าน" enables it, and the chat then explains how to pay in person; the ถุงเงิน QR is never sent in LINE.
  3. Trust at payment: the shop name, the account name to expect, the exact amount under the QR, and the promise that staff confirm by hand.
  4. "โอนแล้ว" only moves the order to รอตรวจสอบ (blue, clock icon). Only staff can make it ชำระแล้ว.
  5. LINE's own header bar (top) is not ours; our page starts below it and respects the bottom safe area (34 px).
